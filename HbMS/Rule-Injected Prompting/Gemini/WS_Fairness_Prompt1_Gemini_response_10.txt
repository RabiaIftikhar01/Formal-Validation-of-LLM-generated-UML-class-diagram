  --

  +calculateFinalBill(bill: Bill, coverage: CoverageDetails): Money

class BillingPolicyFactory {
  --
  +getPolicy(coverage: CoverageDetails): BillingPolicy {static}

note top of BillingPolicy : Strategy Interface:\nDefines contract for applying different\nbilling rules. Enables runtime interchangeability.
note bottom of ChargeItem : Abstract base for all chargeable items.\nEach concrete subclass encapsulates its specific pricing logic.
